Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Valley Motors Kurow ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and handles your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020. This is placeholder content for the demonstration site and should be reviewed by the business before going live.

1. What We Collect

We only collect the information we need to carry out your work: your name, contact phone number, and details about your vehicle and the service requested. We collect this directly from you when you book or drop in.

2. How We Use It

Your information is used to book your vehicle in, carry out and record the work, contact you about your job, and send you an invoice. We may keep a service history for your vehicle so we can help you better next time.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We only share it where necessary to complete your work — for example, with a parts supplier — or where required by law.

4. Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. We keep records only for as long as we need them or as required by law.

5. Your Rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request that we correct it if it is wrong. Contact us and we'll help.

6. Website

This website links out to Google Maps and social media platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

7. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call us on 03 436 0865 or visit us at 14 Wynyard Street, Kurow.
