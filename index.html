About the workshop

The garage the Waitaki Valley relies on

Valley Motors is Kurow's local workshop on Wynyard Street — the handy stop for a WOF, a service or a repair without the drive to a bigger town. We look after the cars, utes and work vehicles this district runs on, and we treat every one like it's got to get someone home tonight.

No jargon, no surprise invoices — just honest work from people who live here too.
