FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, ring Scott and ask.

How do I book my car in?

Ring 027 925 2316 or the workshop on 03 436 0865 and tell us what's going on. We'll line up a time that suits.

When are you open?

Doors open at 8am on weekdays. Ring ahead if you're passing through outside that.

Do you do WOFs?

Give Scott a ring and ask what's covered right now, and if we can't do something ourselves we'll point you to who can.

My car won't start. Can you help?

Ring us first. Often it's a battery, and that's an easy fix. We'll talk it through before you pay for a tow.

What happens after I book?

Drop the car in, we'll confirm what it needs and what it'll cost before we start, then ring you when it's ready.
