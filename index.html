Questions, answered straight

The things people ask us most. Anything else, just ring.

03 436 0865
How do I book a WOF or service?

Give the workshop a ring on 03 436 0865 with your vehicle and a day that suits, and we'll get you booked in.

Can I just drop in?

You're welcome to, but ring first. That way we can make sure someone's on the tools when you arrive at 14 Wynyard Street.

What vehicles do you work on?

Family cars, utes, 4WDs and the farm and work vehicles the Waitaki Valley runs on.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. You get a clear quote first and approve it before we touch the vehicle, so there's no bill shock.

What happens if my car fails its WOF?

We'll show you what failed and why, and quote the fix. Under NZTA rules you have 28 days to get the work done and bring it back for a recheck of the failed items.

My warning light is on. Can you check it?

Yes. We plug in, read the fault code and tell you straight what's going on before it turns into something bigger.

Do you do tyres and wheel alignment?

We do: new tyres, fitting, balancing and wheel alignment for gravel roads and the open road alike.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday during workshop hours. For weekends, ring ahead on 03 436 0865.
